# PROJECT CONTEXT

## Project Name
**AI Gym & Fitness Assistant**

## Vision
Build the world's most intelligent AI fitness ecosystem combining:

- AI Personal Trainer
- Pose Detection & Exercise Recognition
- Diet Planning & AI Nutritionist
- AI Coach & Voice Assistant
- Smart Gym & Wearables Integration
- Recovery AI & Sleep Analysis
- Analytics & Recommendation Engine
- IoT & Computer Vision
- LLM Agent Orchestration

## Target Users

| Segment | Use Case |
|---|---|
| Individual users | Personal fitness tracking & AI coaching |
| Personal trainers | Client management & workout planning |
| Fitness centers | Gym management & smart equipment |
| Corporate wellness | Employee health programs |
| Smart gyms | IoT integration & automation |
| Healthcare partners | Recovery monitoring & prescription |

## Non-Negotiables
- The application **must** be modular, scalable, cloud-native, secure, maintainable, and enterprise-ready.
- Every feature must be independently deployable.
- The system must support **500k–2M+ lines of code** as it grows.

## Key Constraints
- API-first design
- Mobile-first responsive UI
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility
- GDPR & HIPAA-aware data handling
- Multi-tenant architecture support
