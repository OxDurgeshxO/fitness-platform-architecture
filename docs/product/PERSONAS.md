# Athlete & Coach Personas

## Overview
User personas guide kinematic sensitivity, auditory cue density, safety thresholds, and feedback tone across the AI Biomechanical Platform.

---

### Persona 1: Marcus Aurelius — The Progressive Strength Athlete
- **Demographics**: 28 years old, Software Engineer, lifts 4 days per week in a garage gym.
- **Goals**:
  - Break plateau on barbell back squat (working towards 160 kg).
  - Ensure every competition-style rep achieves valid below-parallel depth (femur below horizontal).
  - Avoid lower-back rounding ("butt wink") during heavy single/triple sets.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Filming sets on iPhone requires stopping workout, scrubbing videos manually, and guessing hip angles.
  - Traditional fitness trackers count reps inaccurately or count partial reps as complete reps.
- **Key Needs from Platform**:
  - Strict depth thresholds (only counts reps reaching <95 degrees).
  - Instant audio ping on valid depth so he knows when to reverse movement without checking screen.
  - Eccentric/concentric tempo breakdown and fatigue velocity loss metrics.

---

### Persona 2: Elena Rostova — The Return-to-Sport Athlete
- **Demographics**: 34 years old, Product Lead, recovering from a torn ACL (9 months post-surgery).
- **Goals**:
  - Rebuild bilateral lower-body symmetry and quadriceps hypertrophy safely.
  - Train bodyweight and goblet squats without dynamic knee valgus collapse.
  - Share verified range-of-motion and symmetry logs with her physical therapist.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Constant anxiety about reinjuring graft during deep knee flexion.
  - Generic workout apps do not flag subtle inward knee cave.
- **Key Needs from Platform**:
  - Real-time auditory warning ("Drive knees out!") the instant frontal plane knee collapse exceeds 8%.
  - Bilateral load distribution tracking (compares left vs right hip and knee angles).
  - PDF/JSON medical export for physical therapy review.

---

### Persona 3: David Kim — The Beginner Remote Home Lifter
- **Demographics**: 42 years old, Accountant, working from home with limited equipment (adjustable dumbbells).
- **Goals**:
  - Improve general health, burn fat, and develop foundational movement patterns.
  - Learn proper squat mechanics without feeling intimidated in a public gym.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Unsure how far apart feet should be or whether his back angle is safe.
  - Cannot afford \$120/hr personal trainer sessions.
- **Key Needs from Platform**:
  - Clear visual calibration guide before starting workout (ensures full body is in frame).
  - Simple, encouraging conversational feedback ("Great depth! Keep your chest elevated on the way up.").
  - Low cognitive load: hands-free voice-guided workouts.

---

### Persona 4: Coach Sarah Jenkins (CSCS) — The Remote Strength Coach
- **Demographics**: 31 years old, Head Coach at a boutique strength facility managing 35 remote clients.
- **Goals**:
  - Audit client movement quality objectively without watching 150 raw workout videos daily.
  - Spot longitudinal movement degradation before acute injury occurs.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Low-resolution video uploads recorded from skewed angles make form evaluation subjective and slow.
- **Key Needs from Platform**:
  - Automated kinematic report highlighting form breakdown events (reps 8-10 showed +14 deg lumbar flexion).
  - Raw landmark telemetry access and event timelines.
